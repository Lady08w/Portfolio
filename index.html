WHO I AM

I am a marketing and communications professional with over 5 years of experience in B2B and B2C environments. I specialize in creating end-to-end 360° campaigns and events: from strategy definition and KPIs to the management of graphic pieces, print, merch, logistics and agencies; communication during the event or campaign, metrics review, and final results analysis.


My experience across diverse environments — retail, manufacturing, culture, and small businesses — has given me a broad understanding of brand identity and how to adapt it to very different contexts. I am structured, results-driven, and have a strong creative sensibility, which allows me to create fresh, engaging campaigns focused on achieving results. I work with the Adobe design and editing ecosystem, and use generative AI to produce high-quality visual content. I am a native Spanish speaker, with intermediate French and English.
